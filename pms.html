Role

As the UX Designer responsible for TOTVS PMS SaaS, I integrate deep user research, business strategy, and innovative design to create solutions that impact thousands of users in the hotel industry.

Key activities
  • 01Creation of new features, using user research techniques such as interviews and testing.
  • 02Optimization of existing flows based on metric analysis and user behavior, reducing frustration signals by 40% and increasing usage by over 70%.
  • 03Redesign of the Reservation Creation flow, a core system process, working closely with developers to ensure the best experience within technical limits.
  • 04Creation of new components and contributions to the company’s design system.
Results
